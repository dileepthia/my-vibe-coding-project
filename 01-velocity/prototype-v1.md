## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** Provide more context upfront on what the dashboard is for
- **What confused them:** Was not clear straight-away what metric or data point we were talking about
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** How the fleet would grow in the coming months
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** The intent should have been front and center and then progressively drill down into the details. The dashboard looks cluttered and has too much info upfront.
